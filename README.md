# Duckrooms

_Wander the endless backrooms and piece together the Fragments_

Concept: Brian Becker

Implementation: Adám Brudzewsky & Claude (Opus 4.8)

[Play in your browser now!](https://dragnim.github.io/duckrooms/) Explore the maze of buzzing fluorescent halls, solve the APL puzzles, collect the Fragments, and gather all sixteen apples to escape.

## Gameplay

- Move your duck (🦆) with the arrow keys, numpad, or <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd> (and <kbd>Q</kbd><kbd>E</kbd><kbd>Z</kbd><kbd>C</kbd> for diagonals), or <kbd>I</kbd><kbd>J</kbd><kbd>K</kbd><kbd>L</kbd> (and <kbd>U</kbd><kbd>O</kbd><kbd>M</kbd><kbd>.</kbd> for diagonals).
- On a touchscreen or with a mouse, tap a tile to step toward it, or tap outside the map to step that way — even across an edge.
- Move about the map to uncover it and collect the coloured Fragments.
- When prompted for an APL expression, either type it or click the Fragments and buttons to insert.
- Sealed doors (🔒 on a 🚪) open (🚪) once you have collected all the required Fragments.
- Move across the edges of the map to cross into the neighbouring level.
- Free-standing apples (🍎) are the capstone challenges: like doors, each wears a 🔒 and needs its required Fragments before it will pose its puzzle, but it never blocks your path. Every room holds one. **Gather all sixteen apples to escape.**
- Your progress is saved automatically; use _Restart Game_ to begin afresh.
